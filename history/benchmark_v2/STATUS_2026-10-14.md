## What is already supported

RQ1 is substantially answered: frozen retrieval-profile design changes selected evidence and retrieval effectiveness under the same corpus and embedding model.

RQ2 evidence-drift identification is answered: chunk-level evidence changes materially even when source-level drift is much smaller. A full 192×5 computational replay proof is the next gate.

RQ4 has planned descriptive diagnostics by source family, query type, and language relation. Institutional-document queries show the clearest metadata sensitivity. Cross-language queries also show a larger descriptive metadata gain than same-language queries.

RQ3 downstream answer drift has not yet been run and must not be interpreted from retrieval metrics alone.
